5. About Company

Keep it professional and B2B-focused.

Include:

Company story
Manufacturing experience
Own-brand identity
Product specialization
Location
Manufacturing capabilities
Quality philosophy
Wholesale focus

Avoid making it unnecessarily corporate.

6. Why Choose LBL Hosieries?

Create a visual section:

OWN BRAND

Products manufactured under the company's own brand.

MANUFACTURER

Direct manufacturing rather than simply reselling.

COTTON FOCUS

Highlight cotton-based products where applicable.

BULK SUPPLY

Designed for wholesalers, retailers and distributors.

QUALITY CONTROL

Quality checks throughout production.

CONSISTENT SUPPLY

Focus on repeat wholesale requirements.

Only claim things that the company can actually substantiate.

The key positioning

I would make the website communicate:

“We are not simply selling innerwear. We manufacture our own products and supply them to businesses in bulk.”

That distinction should drive the entire UX.

And because you're already planning a dynamic 3D/parallax website, the strongest visual concept would be to make the homepage flow like:

Fabric → Manufacturing → Product → Quality → Bulk Supply → Enquiry

That gives you a natural story for the 3D effects instead of adding parallax just for decoration.
