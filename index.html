01 / The Origin

The Purity of Shankar-6 Cotton

True innerwear comfort begins long before the needle touches cloth. We select only long-staple Shankar-6 virgin cotton from trusted southern and western farms—revered for its exceptional tensile fiber strength, natural softness, and breathability.

Zero Synthetic Blends • 100% Organic Purity
02 / The Spindle

Ring-Spun into Combed Precision

Short, abrasive fibers are rigorously combed out, leaving only parallel, ultra-fine filaments. Spun into 30s and 40s high-twist compact yarns, this eliminates pilling and ensures a clean, lustrous yarn body that resists fraying.

Super-Combed 30s & 40s Compact Ring-Spun Yarns
03 / The Loom

The Rhythmic Breath of Knitting

Inside our Tiruppur facility, precision 28-gauge circular knitting machines interloop the combed yarns into a seamless single-jersey tubular knit. This preserves the cotton's natural four-way elasticity without requiring suffocating synthetics.

Seamless Single Jersey • 165 to 190 GSM Density
04 / The Touch

Silken Softening & Shrink-Lock

The knitted rolls undergo bio-enzyme washing and gentle felt-calendar compaction. This locks the cotton weave in place, eliminating harsh fabric friction and ensuring less than 3% residual shrinkage across months of daily washing.

Bio-Enzyme Washed • Azo-Free Skin-Friendly Dyes
05 / The Seam

Ergonomic Contouring & Flatlock Stitches

Undergarments must never pinch or chafe. Our master tailors use multi-needle chain stitch machines and Japanese flatlock seams that sit flush against the skin. High-recovery elastic waistbands ensure gentle all-day retention without waist bite.

Anti-Chafe Flatlock Seams • Multi-Needle Elastic Attachment

Chapter 07 • The Tiruppur Mill

Two Decades of Craftsmanship in Asia's Knitwear Capital

Operating out of Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu since 2004, LBL Hosieries has developed deep industrial roots in yarn spinning, fabric knitting, and specialized innerwear garmenting.

Unlike trading companies or brand aggregators, our factory owns the complete production cycle. This vertical integrity guarantees wholesale buyers consistent fabric weight, zero color fading, and rapid turnaround for massive seasonal shipments.
